01

Initiate Deletion Request

Log in to your Graxion Flow dashboard, navigate to Settings > Privacy & Data Controls, and click "Request Account & Data Deletion".

02

Ownership Verification

A secure verification challenge is dispatched to your registered primary email. Confirming the code ensures only authorized workspace administrators can trigger data removal.

03

Instant Quarantine

Upon verification or Meta App removal callback, your account is immediately disabled. All active AI bots, webhooks, dispatches, and connected accounts (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram) are instantly disconnected.

04

30-Day Recovery Grace Period

Your data is securely isolated in encrypted cold storage for 30 days. You can restore your workspace anytime during this window by contacting support.

05

Permanent Purge

After 30 days, all conversation logs, media attachments, auth tokens, customer records, and analytics are permanently and irreversibly purged from our database.
