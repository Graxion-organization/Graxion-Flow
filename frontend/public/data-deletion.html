Data Deletion Policy

Follow these steps to permanently delete your WhatsAgent account and all associated data.

01

Request Deletion

Log in to your WhatsAgent dashboard and navigate to Settings > Danger Zone. Click on "Request Account Deletion".

02

Security Verification

For your protection, we will send 3 unique verification codes to your registered email. You must enter all codes in the correct order.

03

Provide Feedback

Select the reason for leaving and provide any feedback. This helps us improve our service for others.

04

Instant Disable

Once verified, your account is immediately disabled. All active AI bots, automation tasks, and agents will stop instantly.

05

30-Day Grace Period

Your data is kept for 30 days. You can restore your account anytime during this period. After 30 days, all data is purged permanently.

You can manage your account and data privacy directly from your user dashboard.
